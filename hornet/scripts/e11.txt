Lee: Lincoln is not from Mecklenburg. Why is he at our table? Is he a guest with muddy shoes too?

Bell: No blood from this river. He is in this show because he learned like our meeting house: few books, copied, recited. Euclid. Shakespeare. The Bible. Blackstone. Slow to scratch, slow to forget. Davidson’s library and UNC’s library are full of books. Lincoln’s library was his knees and a fire. That is why we put him with our people: not blood, method.

Vee: That makes me want to copy something right now. May I use the floor?

Bell: Use the floor. Use a knee. Use a slate. He said the Lyceum must be law, not the mob. Our general died at a ford under law, not under a riot. Films do not pass a Lincoln class. Neither do cartoons, unless you copy. If you only watch this cartoon, you have not done the episode. If you copy one proposition, you have.

Lee: I will copy one proposition. I will hate it and then I will know it. That is the whole method.

Bell: That is the whole method. The bigger school on this git uses it. You are already enrolled. Not certified. Enrolled. Copy one sentence from Euclid or from a speech. Recite it tomorrow. Then this episode counted, and Lincoln would have tipped his hat to a Mecklenburg bell.

Vee: I will recite mine to the bell. I have been practicing whisper and kitchen and pegs. Now I will practice a line I do not yet love.

Lee: I copied a line in my head. I will write it when the coat comes off, because the sleeves eat pencils.

Bell: I am listening. I have been listening since Hopewell hung me. He had no Nassau Hall. He had no Davidson. He made a college of firelight. When you are older, walk a library. Davidson’s. Chapel Hill’s. A fire is a library if you copy. Lincoln would have understood a meeting-house bell. We understand a boy with three books. That is the friendship.

Vee: Copy. Recite. Demonstrate. I like demonstrate because it means show me with your hands.

Bell: Show me with your hands. Show me with a line you can still say in the morning. We do not make a war of this episode. We make a desk of knees. Slow to scratch, slow to forget. The Lyceum address said law must be the political religion. Our general at the ford was law, not a riot. Keep those in one house.

Bell: Good scholars. Sit by the fire one more minute. Three books. A knee for a desk. Euclid that you half hate until it stays. Shakespeare said aloud until it is yours. The Bible. Blackstone. Slow to scratch, slow to forget. He had no Nassau, no Davidson, no Crowfield. He made a college of firelight. Our libraries at Davidson and Chapel Hill are full, and they still only work if you copy. The Lyceum said law must be the political religion, not the mob. Our general at the ford was law, not a riot. We do not make a war of this episode. We make a friendship of method. Lincoln would have understood a meeting-house bell. We understand a boy with three books.

Lee: I will not skip the copy. Watching is not the class. I will write it when the coat comes off.

Vee: I will not skip the recite. The bell is the teacher who will not let me mumble. Copy. Recite. Demonstrate — show me with your hands.

Bell: Copy tonight: Lincoln had no college. He copied Euclid, Shakespeare, the Bible, Blackstone. Recite: slow to scratch, slow to forget. You are already enrolled. Not certified. Enrolled. Ring once. Tomorrow a wagon, straw, a broken wheel in Bethlehem, and a North Carolina colonel who chose to keep a voice instead of making a gun. Bring your quietest love. I will bring the cousin in Philadelphia.

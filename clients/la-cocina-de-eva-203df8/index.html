Our story

Serving the Lord by serving you.

Eva and Edie opened La Cocina de Eva in 2001 with one mission: high-quality food, always fresh and never canned, served with a warm welcome. More than two decades later, it's still family run on Main Street.

Banquet room for family and work parties, with high-capacity seating.

What's here

Everything you need for wash day

No surprises and no babysitting a broken machine. Just a well-kept laundromat on the edge of Concho, open every day of the week.

Washers & dryers

Self-serve machines kept in top shape, so your clothes come out clean and dry the first time and you aren't feeding quarters into a dryer that won't dry.

Hot private showers

Clean, private showers with real hot water and good pressure. Pay for your time, get cleaned up, and get back on the road or back to camp. Ask the attendant for today's rate.

Clean, quiet & friendly

Spotless floors and folding tables, a calm place to wait, and friendly staff who keep it that way.

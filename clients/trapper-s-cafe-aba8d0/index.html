Our story

A Taylor tradition since 1973.

"Trapper" Hatch opened this family diner in 1973 and decorated it with his old trapping gear and paintings of local wildlife by area artists. Locals and travelers on the highway still stop for a slice at the counter with a cup of coffee.

Whole pies to order for Thanksgiving, Christmas and every family get-together.
Daily specials on the chalkboard, like grilled ham and Swiss on rye.

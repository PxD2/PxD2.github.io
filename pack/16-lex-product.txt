ONE LINE
Store and send a 1-, 2-, or 4-byte ideogram. Expand it to a paragraph only where the book already lives.

THE MATH (CLOSED BOOK)
  1 byte   → up to 256 paragraphs
  2 bytes  → up to 65,536 paragraphs
  4 bytes  → up to 4,294,967,296 paragraphs
A 500-byte status line becomes 1 byte + optional slots after both ends hold the same library hash.
That is the 99%+ cut. It is not gzip for arbitrary English.

ANCESTORS, NOT COMPETITORS
  Q-code     three letters = a sentence on Morse
  HTTP 404   three digits = a full error sentence
  LEX        1/2/4 bytes = a versioned paragraph + slots, hashed to the book

DYNAMIC MODE
  Minting a new paragraph assigns a new id and ships a library revision out of band.
  Do not put the novel paragraph on the radio and call it 4-byte compression.

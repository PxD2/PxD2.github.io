PXD2 Game Test

Play PXD2 games before anyone else, right in your browser. The game runs on PXD2's own GPUs and streams to you, so there's nothing to install and the game's code never leaves PXD2.

Checking for an open test…Open the test

How it works

  1. PXD2 sends you an invite code.
  2. Open the test, press Join, and enter the code. You get your own tester pass, saved in your browser.
  3. Press Play now. The game streams to your screen; your keyboard, mouse or controller drive it.
  4. Found a bug or have an idea? Send it from the same page, with a screenshot.

What you get

Your whole menu, on every phone

Every item and price, split into your menus and sections, with search. Customers tap Add, write a special request, and see their total as they go.

  • Breakfast before 11, lunch and dinner after, automatically
  • We load it from your current online menu or a photo of your printed one

Two ways to pay

Pay at pickup: the order goes straight to your kitchen with a name, phone and pickup time. Pay now: the customer checks out with the card provider you already use, like Toast, Square or Clover, so your card fees and payouts stay exactly as they are.

Orders land on a ticket screen

Any tablet in your kitchen becomes the order board. New orders chime and flash. Tap once to start cooking, once for ready, once when it's picked up. Late orders turn red.

  • Shows pay-at-counter or paid online on every ticket
  • Runs in the tablet's web browser, no app to install

An events calendar you run from your phone

Live music, karaoke, taco Tuesday, holiday hours. Post it from your phone in a few taps and it's on your site right away, with a month calendar and an "Add to my calendar" button for your customers.

  • Weekly events repeat on their own
  • Nothing to log in to but your own phone

PXD2 Access · built in, not bolted on

A web for everyone. Hands, voice or ears.

The ADA is our floor, not our finish line. Every PXD2 site is built from the first line of code to WCAG 2.2 AA, meets the stricter AAA level wherever it can — contrast, text size, spacing, no time limits — and is built to keep pace with WCAG 3 as it's finalized. Then PXD2 Access goes further: speak to navigate, listen to any page, and find your way by tone.

Access Alt + A

Press a button, or press Alt + A from anywhere on the page.

Voice, no hands

Say where you want to go, what to click, or what to type. Runs on your device — your voice isn't recorded or sold.

Read aloud

Any page, any section, read in a natural voice at your speed — and it works alongside screen readers, not over them.

Tone guide

Short, distinct tones tell blind and low-vision visitors where they are — a rising tone for a heading, a click for a link, a chime for a button.

Easy on the eyes

Warm, low-glare colors, high-contrast text, larger type and calm motion by default — and it follows your device's settings.

Our accessibility promise

  • Every site works by keyboard, touch, voice, mouse and screen reader.
  • Every image has a description; every video has captions.
  • Every site is tested by hand and with real assistive technology — never left to a widget.
  • Every barrier you report gets a reply within two business days and a fix, at no charge.

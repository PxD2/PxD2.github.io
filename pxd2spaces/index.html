The field — start at 0–250 g

We own the dirt. We do not own the sky. Drones are the majority use. Rockets are a posted exception. One activity at a time.

Fun fleet

Sub-250 g, flown only for fun, in this Class G: no ATC, no FAA registration, no Remote ID. TRUST is still required. Weight is at takeoff — battery, camera, tape.

Work fleet

The same 249 g quad used to train pilots for work is Part 107. Register that airframe. Broadcast Remote ID even under 250 g. Do not mix the boxes.

  • Concho Fire District is volunteer. Station 1: 20 County Road 5069. Non-emergency 928-337-2681. Emergency 911. Ask them to stand by on rocket days. They may be on a medical. You are the first engine.
  • Red-flag / Stage 2: no torch, no motor. 3.3 miles behind Concho Elementary is not a waiver.
  • Ultralight: Part 103, daylight, not over settlement. Keep the FAA note in writing.

    House rules

    1. Not a business. No insurance. You enter at your own risk. A signed waiver is required. A waiver is not immunity.
    2. Parent or legal guardian in the bay with their own child. No stand-ins. Steward on duty for torch, blades, and range, or those tools stay locked.
    3. Volunteer fire only. Ask Concho Fire to stand by on rocket days. Water on site anyway. No torch or launch on red-flag / Stage 2.
    4. Class G field: one activity at a time, posted. We schedule the dirt, not the sky. Pattern off the school and the houses.
    5. Sub-250 g fun fleet: no ATC, no registration, no Remote ID. TRUST required. Weight at takeoff.
    6. Training pilots for work is Part 107 even under 250 g.
    7. No PVC in a laser. No resin without vent. No tool overnight loans in year 1.
    8. Student files on the Nation stay with the student.

    • Scopes, filament, rod. Instruction is free; stock is not.

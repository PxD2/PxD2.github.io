HORNET BELL — Episode 12: Polk Saves the Bell

Hopewell. Vee asks Bell if she is the Liberty Bell from Philadelphia. Bell says she is her cousin in the story.

Philadelphia, September 1777. British coming. Bells make cannon if you melt them. Colonel Thomas Polk of Charlotte is tasked to get the city’s bells, including the State House Bell, out in wagons under straw.

Night road to Allentown. North Carolina Continentals walk beside the wagons. Lanterns. No battle.

24 September 1777. Allentown. The bells are still bells.

William Polk, later lieutenant colonel, is in that same North Carolina line.

Catawba pines. Vee and Lee listen. Bell: one Polk house kept my cousin from becoming a gun. James Knox Polk will sit in the White House from this family.

01Situation

The fight for the straits

US forces flying and sailing PXD2-designed systems face the Meridian Coalition's navy and air force for control of the chokepoints that move the world's shipping. The maps are naval-sim large. The action stays fast and readable, with clean explosions and destruction you fight through.

Mud for Blood is the story campaign. It leads into online multiplayer on the same full-size maps, with community dedicated servers the way PC shooters used to run them.

02Hero map

Tunnel Rats

A modern tunnel-warfare battlefield: close, dark, loud and unlike anything else in the genre. Above ground, long-range sniping rewards patience over twitch reflexes, so veteran players have a place to shine.

More theaters in the current build: Battle for LA · Fight for Japan · Operation Iron Furrow · Bandar · Drone Hunter. Modes: Mud for Blood campaign, fleet battles, FPS, and Battle Royale with a B-52 drop and ram-air parachute.

03The studio

One developer. No publisher.

PXD2 is Chad Peters: software developer, PCB and embedded engineer, and solo game builder, working from a fully off-grid studio in Concho, Arizona. Every ship, sub and drone was modeled in Blender and brought into Unreal Engine. The first playable build came together in about a week, and early public gameplay drew a strong response.

GLOBAL THEATERS is planned as a franchise. Vietnam and Global War on Terror titles follow, each kept focused, moddable and playable on ordinary PCs.

06Build-out

Real props, real scans

Funding expands the studio itself. New workstations and 3D scanners let PXD2 scan real military props and gear into the game, and 3D printers turn those same scans into physical props for trailers and film productions. The same pipeline, with AI tools on top, speeds up new theaters and DLC.

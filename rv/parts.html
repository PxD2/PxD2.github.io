3D scan the broken piece. Print the next one.

Dealer plastic for a 12-year-old fifth-wheel is often a two-week wait. We scan the cracked cap, handle, sensor mount, vent lid, or drawer latch on the site or at the Show Low bench, print a fit part, and bag it with the tank ticket.

Good print jobs

Bayonet caps, compartment knobs, tank-valve handles, sensor brackets, vent lids, shower-door guides, broken interior tabs. Not pressure-bearing gas fittings. Not legal hitch or brake parts.

How it is priced

Small scan + print from $35. Larger housings quoted after the scan. Park accounts can batch several sites in one print run.

Where the files live

Working CAD is on the PXD2 floor. Start at pxd2.github.io/cad if you already have a scan or a photo set.

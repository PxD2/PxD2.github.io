The map is the movie

Where you actually stand

Every stop is tied to Star Wars or Indiana Jones production. Galaxy Tours was first to fold verified location-scout sites into a commercial itinerary — not just the famous finished sets.

Lars Homestead

Chott el-Djerid salt flats near Nefta. Binary sunset. Moisture farm. The 50th-anniversary concert and World Origin Site plaque live here.

Mos Espa

Eriguet dunes outside Nefta. Podrace streets, Anakin’s hovel, and the occasional real sandstorm — no CGI required.

Hotel Sidi Driss

Matmata. The interior of the Lars homestead, carved into the earth. Cosplay backdrop of Star Wars Celebration fame.

Amghar Mosque

Djerba. Obi-Wan Kenobi’s original house. Heritage site and a 2027 eclipse vantage on the island circuit.

Sidi Jemour / Tosche Station

Djerba’s west coast. Chosen for the 2027 totality window — more than four minutes of darkness on a film site.

Raiders Tunisia

Kairouan rooftops (Sallah), canyon and desert action country, and the southern locations that set the tone for the Indiana Jones franchise.

Who runs the expedition

Founded by three brothers. Built in Tunisia.

Galaxy Tours is the company dedicated to Lucasfilm tourism in Tunisia — not a sideline desert day-trip bolted onto a generic coach tour.

Creator and CEO Davin Anderson spent years on primary-source research, field work, and guide training before the first guest departed in October 2023. The team now includes specialized Tunisian guides based in Djerba, Tozeur, Kairouan, and Tunis, plus collaborators in the United States and Europe. Tours run in English; French, German, Italian, and Arabic are available by request.

They are a registered ONTT operator, insured by AIC, and the only travel company Galaxy Tours publicly states is approved by Lucasfilm to exhibit at Star Wars Celebration. In 2025 they ran 64 tours for 174 guests and opened an on-location store at Dar Hi in Nefta.

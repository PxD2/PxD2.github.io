PXD2

Ask for a part

Reverse-engineering CAD for a child-simple shop. Stamp a plate. Fire a laser through the holes. Align. Print on a Creality K2 Plus 350³. Kernel is millimeters. Display inches if you want.

Laser level

False 3-plane lasers through every hole, like a Bosch on the bench. Red along X, green plumb on Y, cyan along Z. Drag until the beams coincide. Align holes slides the selected part onto the other part’s pattern.

Lock X Lock Y Lock Z

Side X

Horizontal

A red beam through the hole, spanning the bed. Line two plates left-right until the X lasers are one line.

Vert Y

Plumb

Green up the hole axis. Scroll-wheel lifts the part on its vertical beds. Magnets onto nearby stack tops.

Side Z

Depth

Cyan through the hole front-to-back. Rulers on the bed. Dimension brackets on the selected solid.

The tray

Layers pile like paint. Stamp, stack, drag, arrows. Flip and rotate on gamer-mouse side buttons — back is flip 90°, forward is rotate 90°. F and R if you only have a laptop.

Ask

Say the part

Type or speak. “80×60 plate 6 mm thick with 4 M6 holes.” Local parser or Grok. NEMA faces, pulleys, 608s, GoPro mounts — real solids, not lookalike plates.

Thingiverse

Twins that fit

Premade hits land as the correct kind. A NEMA 17 is 42.3 with a 31 mm bolt pattern. The laser fires through those holes.

Units

mm · cm · in

Kernel stays millimeters. Display whatever the shop uses. Inches for the ranch. Metric for the printer.

Export

STL and friends

STL, DXF, OpenSCAD, CadQuery, FreeCAD, Blender, JSON. Peg-split what will not fit the K2.

Generic Revo

A generic Revopoint path that goes direct to STL. Drop a scan. Voxelize. Stamp it on the tray. Move it. Print it.

Drop

Cloud to solid

PLY / mesh in. Occupancy mesh out. The Generic device auto-stamps onto the bed so you can drag it like any other layer.

Shop

Reverse-eng

Scan the ranch part. Align holes to a NEMA face or an M6 plate. That is the whole point of the laser.
